This policy sets out the circumstances in which *we* will support and request a

In practice, when a request meets this policy, we approve it and it is

## 3. Mirror Pro

**All sales are final.** A Pro charge, whether it is your first or a renewal,
is not refundable as a matter of course.

**Cancel before the renewal date and the charge never happens.** That is the
control this policy asks you to use, and it is always available to you: one
click in the customer portal, at any point in the period you have already paid
for. Nothing is charged after you cancel.

**The exception is technical failure**, and it applies to Pro exactly as it
applies to the one-time purchase — see §4. If you paid and the product does not
work, that is not a change of mind and we will fix it or refund you.

If a renewal genuinely caught you out — you meant to cancel and did not — write
to us anyway. We are not obliged to refund it and this policy does not promise
that we will, but we would still rather hear from you than take money from
someone who does not want the product.

**Cancelling is not the same as a refund.** Cancelling stops future charges and
leaves you with access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
Asking for a refund asks for money back and ends access now. If you only want
to stop being charged, cancel — you do not need this policy. You can cancel
from the Polar customer portal, linked in your receipt, or by emailing
us.

**Partial periods are not pro-rated.** If you cancel mid-period you keep access
to the end of it rather than receiving part of the payment back.

## 4. The $1 Starter Pack, and the technical-fault exception

**The $1 Starter Pack is not refundable.**

It grants permanent, non-expiring access to the five starter scenes, and it is
delivered in full the moment it is paid for. A refund after delivery would mean
returning the money while the access remains granted, so it is priced as a
one-way purchase and sold at $1 on that basis.

**The exception is technical failure.** If the purchase does not grant the
access it should — the scenes stay locked, a payment succeeded but nothing
unlocked, the entitlement is missing or wrong — that is our fault, not a change
of mind. Tell us and we will either fix the access or refund you, whichever you
prefer. We would rather fix it, because you wanted the scenes.

Nothing here affects any statutory right of withdrawal you have as a consumer
(§6).

## 5. How to ask

Either path works, and you do not need to try both:

- **Email contact@mirrorspeak.app.** Say what you bought and roughly when.
  Your MIRROR username or the email you bought with is enough to find the
  order. This is the better route if something is broken, because we can fix
  it as well as refund it.
- **Use your Polar receipt.** Every purchase receipt links to a
  customer portal where you can manage or cancel the subscription and contact
  support. Polar handles order support as merchant of record.

We aim to reply within 3 business days. We will tell you the outcome either
way, and if we decline a request we will say why.

Once a refund is approved, the time it takes to reach your account is set by
your bank or card issuer, typically 5–10 business days.

**Please write to us before opening a chargeback.** A chargeback costs us a fee
and takes longer for you than a refund we agree to. If this policy covers your
request, asking directly is faster.

## 6. Your statutory rights

If you are a consumer in the UK, the EU, or another jurisdiction with mandatory
consumer protection law, you may have a statutory right to cancel a purchase
within a set period — and in some cases that right is stronger than this
policy.

**This policy does not reduce those rights.** Where your local law gives you a
right to cancel or withdraw that §3 and §4 do not offer, your legal right
applies and we will honour it regardless of what this policy says. Write to us
and say so.

This matters here: "all sales final" is our policy, not an override of your
statutory rights, and it is not a waiver of them.
